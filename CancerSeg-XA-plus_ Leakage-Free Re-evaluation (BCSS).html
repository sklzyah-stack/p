03 · KEY SLIDE

The splitting problem

Original protocol: random patch split

~50% overlapping tiles, shuffled at random. Same-patient look-alikes land in train AND test.

Our protocol: patient-level split

Patients are split FIRST, then patches are extracted. No patient is ever in two sets.

Honest estimate of performance on NEW patients, which is what matters clinically.

09 · CONTEXT

Context: the published numbers

Paper's reported CancerSeg-XA (random split)

Published numbers use a random patch split with overlapping patches and are NOT directly comparable to patient-level results. A lower accuracy under patient-level evaluation reflects a stricter, more realistic test, not a weaker model.

11 · CONCLUSION

Three takeaways

1

Trustworthy estimate

Leakage-free evaluation gives an estimate we can trust on new patients.

2

Measured directly

In an identical environment, the effect of CBAM + skip attention is measured directly.

Future work

Multi-seed runs, more datasets, a lighter backbone.
